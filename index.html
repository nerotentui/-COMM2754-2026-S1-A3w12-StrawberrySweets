SDG 15: Life On Land

Protect, restore and promote sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems, sustainably manage forests, combat desertification, and halt and reverse land degradation and halt biodiversity loss.

"we damage the natural world, we damage ourselves…We have the power. We have the knowledge to actually live in harmony with nature."

- David Attenborough, WEF 2019

As humans, we are the only species that have the ability to destroy the planet and capable of restoring it. We must protect our Earth before it's too late. Every action counts toward rewilding our home. One of the big reasons why we are in this situation is because of deforestation. We destroy the nature that gives us life to use that land for buildings, agriculture, and other human activities. We need to stop deforestation and start reforestation, plant more trees, protect existing forests, and restore degraded lands.
